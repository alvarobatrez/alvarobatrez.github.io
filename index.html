    Full research log
    01

    Evolved synaptic plasticity

    Local plasticity rules — R-STDP and BTSP families, modulated by reward prediction error — discovered by evolution rather than designed by hand.

    02

    Spiking neural networks

    Brain-inspired controllers for continuous control and locomotion — matching ANNs on quadruped tasks while opening the door to neuromorphic deployment.

    03

    Embodied damage recovery

    Quadrupeds that lose an actuator and recover locomotion in seconds — re-learning in the body, without retraining episodes and without catastrophic forgetting.
