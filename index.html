    Research interests

    01

    Trustworthy AI

    Robustness, reliability and safety of learning systems — understanding when and why models fail, and how to make failure modes visible before deployment.

    02

    General artificial intelligence

    Toward systems that transfer and reason beyond their training distribution; the architectures, objectives and evaluation methods that get us closer.

    03

    Simulation & experimentation

    Computational experiments as a research instrument: building controlled environments where hypotheses about intelligent behaviour can be tested and measured.
